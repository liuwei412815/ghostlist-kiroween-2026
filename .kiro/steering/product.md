# GhostList product direction

These are project instructions prepared before a Kiro development session.
They are not a transcript or proof of Kiro use.

GhostList makes small tasks feel approachable. Its audience is people who have
several small unfinished things on their minds and want a gentle next step.

- Treat tasks as friendly ghosts. Avoid guilt, shame, punishment and streak loss.
- Keep the create-focus-complete-restore loop obvious.
- Use English UI text that sounds like a helpful human.
- Store task text locally. Do not add analytics, accounts or external AI calls.
- Preserve user data when evolving the schema.
- Support keyboard users, small screens and reduced motion.
- Keep optional features out of the main path until the user needs them.
